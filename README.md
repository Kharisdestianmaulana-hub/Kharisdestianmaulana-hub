## English

### About Me

In a typical week, I might be fixing the architecture of a native macOS video editor in the morning, writing a horror game script in the afternoon, then working on the system design for a service studio I am building with friends at night. It is not a lack of focus, it is because for me all of it comes from the same impulse: building systems and building stories. Sometimes it takes the shape of code, sometimes the shape of narrative. The engineer side and the writer side are not competing with each other, they are both about building a world with its own consistent rules.

I would rather open the editor and start building than spend too long planning on paper. That is also why I tend to run several projects in parallel instead of one at a time.

When it comes to how I work and how I design, I lean minimalist: flat, clean, no emoji, and a preference for dark or cream palettes that feel deliberate rather than default.

### Education & Organization

- IT Student at **Universitas Catur Insan Cendikia (UCIC)**, Cirebon
- At **HIMATIF UCIC** (Informatics Engineering Student Association), I am part of Division 2, the Training and Development Department, which focuses on developing and training IT students

### What I Do

I run **Lokara Studio**, where I develop games and tools based on my own vision, including building a custom game engine forked from Godot. Alongside that, I am co-founding **Kodein**, a web development and design service studio with friends, working on everything from branding to platform architecture.

Outside of those two, I experiment a lot with native macOS projects, from small productivity tools to more complex apps like a video editor, along with projects around systems and infrastructure, including building my own Linux distro.

### Currently Working On

**[Kino](https://github.com/Kharisdestianmaulana-hub/Kino)**
It started out of curiosity about what it takes to build a video editor from scratch, not to compete with Final Cut Pro or Premiere on features, but so that one day I have my own video editor and do not have to rely on other people's tools. Kino is a native non-linear video editor for macOS, built entirely with Swift, SwiftUI, and AVFoundation, with a filesystem-first approach where original media is never copied into a hidden library, and a modular architecture that fully follows the Command Pattern for undo/redo.
`Swift` `SwiftUI` `AVFoundation` `macOS`

**[Notchify](https://github.com/Kharisdestianmaulana-hub/notchify)**
The idea was simple: I wanted the Dynamic Island experience, but my Mac does not have a notch or that feature. So I built it myself. Notchify brings an interactive Dynamic Island style overlay to any Mac, complete with a set of modular mini-apps (media player, clipboard manager, pomodoro timer, system monitor, and a daily agenda) with full bilingual support.
`SwiftUI` `AppKit` `macOS`

### Tentang Saya

Dalam satu minggu biasa, saya bisa membenahi arsitektur video editor native macOS di pagi hari, menulis skrip game horor di sore hari, lalu malamnya balik lagi ngerjain desain sistem untuk studio jasa yang sedang saya bangun bareng teman. Bukan karena nggak fokus, tapi karena buat saya, semua itu sebenarnya satu impuls yang sama: membangun sistem dan membangun cerita. Kadang wujudnya kode, kadang wujudnya narasi. Sisi engineer dan sisi penulis bukan dua hal yang bersaing, keduanya sama-sama soal membangun dunia yang punya aturan sendiri dan konsisten.

Saya lebih senang langsung buka editor dan mulai membangun daripada berlama-lama merancang di atas kertas. Itu juga alasan kenapa saya lebih sering punya proyek yang berjalan paralel daripada satu per satu.

Soal cara kerja dan preferensi visual, saya orangnya minimalis: flat, bersih, tanpa emoji, dan lebih suka palet gelap atau krem yang terasa deliberate, bukan default template.

### Sedang Dikerjakan

**[Kino](https://github.com/Kharisdestianmaulana-hub/Kino)**
Awalnya saya cuma penasaran gimana rasanya bikin video editor dari nol, bukan buat menyaingi Final Cut Pro atau Premiere secara fitur, tapi supaya suatu saat saya punya video editor sendiri dan nggak perlu bergantung ke tools orang lain. Kino jadi video editor non-linear native untuk macOS, dibangun sepenuhnya dengan Swift, SwiftUI, dan AVFoundation, dengan pendekatan filesystem-first, di mana media asli tidak pernah disalin ke library tersembunyi, dan arsitektur modular yang mengikuti Command Pattern penuh untuk undo/redo.
`Swift` `SwiftUI` `AVFoundation` `macOS`

**[Notchify](https://github.com/Kharisdestianmaulana-hub/notchify)**
Ide awalnya sederhana: saya pengen pengalaman ala Dynamic Island, tapi Mac saya nggak punya notch atau fitur itu. Jadi saya bikin sendiri. Notchify membawa overlay interaktif ala Dynamic Island ke Mac mana pun, lengkap dengan kumpulan mini-apps modular (media player, clipboard manager, pomodoro timer, system monitor, hingga agenda harian) dengan dukungan dwibahasa penuh.
`SwiftUI` `AppKit` `macOS`
